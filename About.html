About Nantara

Good businesses deserve better ways of working.

We build AI workflows for the operational work that keeps business moving. Starting with the journey from purchase order to sales order.

Why we exist

The work between systems is still someone’s full-time job.

Behind every sales order is a person reading messages, checking a catalogue, confirming a price and typing the same details again. The systems are there. The gaps between them are where the work piles up.

Nantara starts in that gap. We work with distributors, wholesalers, importers and trading companies to design order workflows around their actual documents, rules and people.

Our approach is practical: understand the process, test a focused pilot, measure the outcome and earn the right to expand.
