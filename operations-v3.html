The operational bottleneck

Your ERP is not the problem. The work before it is.

Orders arrive in different formats and channels. Someone still has to read them, check the details and type them into the system. That work slows down the order desk and makes mistakes easy to miss.

01

Orders are scattered

WhatsApp messages, email threads, PDFs, spreadsheets, screenshots and salespeople all feed the same order process.

02

Checks depend on memory

Admins verify customers, SKUs, quantities, price lists, stock, credit status and approvals one order at a time.

03

The same data is typed again

A purchase order becomes another spreadsheet row, another ERP entry and another follow-up. Delays and duplicate work compound as volume grows.

The opportunity is not to replace your ERP. It is to remove the repetitive work between receiving a purchase order and creating a reliable sales order.

Human control by design

Routine work moves. Exceptions stop for review.

Automation should not turn an unclear PO into a wrong order. Nantara separates work that can proceed from work that requires a decision, using the rules agreed for your operation.

AutoA clear order passes the required checks and follows the approved path.
ReviewA price mismatch, unclear SKU or stock issue goes to your team.
BlockA transaction cannot move forward until the required approval is recorded.

Rules, permissions and integration scope are defined during implementation.

A practical scenario

A purchase order arrives with 18 line items.

The Order Agent matches the customer and checks each item against the agreed catalogue and rules. Sixteen lines are clear. One price differs from the agreed terms, and one item needs a stock check. Those two issues go to an admin. The sales order waits until they are resolved.

FAQ

Questions before we look at your workflow.

Is this a WhatsApp chatbot?

No. WhatsApp can be one source of purchase orders. The Order Agent is built around reading, validating and processing orders across the agreed channels and systems.

Does Nantara replace our ERP?

No. The aim is to reduce the manual work before a sales order reaches your existing ERP or accounting workflow. Integration scope depends on your system.

What happens when a price or SKU is wrong?

The agent flags the mismatch under the rules we agree with your team. A person reviews or approves the exception before the order proceeds.

Can it handle PDFs, spreadsheets and email?

Those are common starting formats. During discovery we review your real samples, file quality and channel access to define what the pilot can support.

Will it post orders without approval?

Only if your approved workflow allows that for a clearly defined case. You decide which actions run automatically and which require review.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the number of order formats, rules, integrations and approvals. We scope a pilot and timeline after reviewing your process.

What does pricing include?

Pricing is scoped to the implementation, ongoing operation and any third-party usage. We provide a written proposal after understanding the workflow and integration requirements.

How do we know it is working?

We agree on a baseline and review measures such as handling time, manual touches, exceptions and avoidable order errors during the pilot.

What about data access and security?

We define access, permissions, data handling and the systems involved for each implementation. Ask us to review your requirements before any integration is connected.
