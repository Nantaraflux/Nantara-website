A few things worth asking

Let’s make it clear.

Is Nantara a WhatsApp chatbot? +

WhatsApp is one way orders arrive. Our focus is the work after that: reading the PO, validating its details and preparing a sales order. Email, PDF and spreadsheet inputs can also be included in an agreed workflow.

Do we need to replace our ERP? +

No. Nantara is designed to connect the order intake process with your existing system. We first assess available APIs, import options and access permissions. Supported actions depend on that assessment.

What happens when an order is unclear? +

Missing information, uncertain matches and rule conflicts are flagged for your team. You decide which cases can proceed automatically and which must wait for approval.

How do we start, and what does it cost? +

We review sample purchase orders, your current process and system access. Then we propose a scoped pilot with written pricing, a timeline and success measures. There is no one-size-fits-all deployment promise.

How is business data handled? +

Before a pilot, we agree on the data required, who can access it, which systems and providers are involved, and how it should be retained. Share your security and data requirements during discovery so they can be assessed before connecting systems.
