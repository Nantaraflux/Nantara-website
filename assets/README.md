# Nantara editorial design assets

## Original illustration

- `nantara-river-original.png`: original full-resolution artwork generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 20 September 2026. No API fallback or third-party reference assets were used.
- `nantara-river.webp`: optimized web encoding of the same illustration. Used for the homepage landscape, About image and consultation section.
- Concept: Indonesian waterways connecting into one flow, representing the transition from scattered incoming orders to a structured sales order. This is conceptual brand artwork, not a photograph of a customer or facility.

### Generation prompt

Create one original premium editorial landscape illustration for Nantara, a B2B operations software company from Indonesia. Wide panoramic aspect ratio 3:2, high resolution. A tranquil Indonesian archipelago river valley viewed from a slightly elevated viewpoint: a winding jade river flows from distant misty indigo volcanic hills toward the foreground, terraced tropical foothills and soft pale palms on the banks, a very small tasteful timber trade pavilion and simple jetty at far right, a small cargo boat subtly in the mid-distance. The central concept is many tributaries joining into one clear flow, a metaphor for structured business orders. Sophisticated hand-painted gouache and colored pencil texture on fine cream paper, matte editorial art, restrained sage green, dusty teal, deep ink blue, pale lilac distant mountains and warm ivory sky, tiny warm apricot accents. Elegant quiet daylight, expansive airy sky across top third, rich detailed foreground foliage in lower corners, no neon, no sci-fi, no gradients pretending to be tech, no 3D render. Compose with plenty of clear sky at upper center, river opening up in lower center, natural asymmetry. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no watermarks. This is a distinctive original illustration, not a reproduction of any reference artwork. Output a polished finished artwork suitable for a luxury editorial website hero.

## Brand and interface

The existing Nantara logo is retained. Product examples, flow diagrams and Web Builder concepts are original HTML/CSS, with bilingual text. Data and company names in the order walkthrough are fictional and labeled as illustrative. No customer results, partner certifications or third-party product screenshots are presented as Nantara evidence.

## Typography

`dm-sans-latin.woff2` is DM Sans, hosted locally to avoid an external font request. Source: Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com, DM Sans v17). The SIL Open Font License is included in `DM-Sans-LICENSE.txt`.

## Design reference

https://duna.com/ was studied for editorial spacing, restrained typography and the transition between illustration and content. All Nantara copy, illustration and interface layouts were created for this site; no Duna assets or source code were copied.

### Harbor prompt

Create one original premium editorial illustration for Nantara, an Indonesian B2B operations company. Very wide panoramic landscape composition, aspect ratio 3:2. Quiet working Indonesian maritime harbor inspired by Sulawesi coastal trading towns, not an exact real location: two elegant traditional wooden phinisi cargo boats moored alongside a stone quay, beautiful distinctive tall twin masts and furled cream sails, weathered teak hulls, small terracotta-roofed Indonesian waterfront warehouses, subtle stacks of woven baskets and wooden crates on the quay. Tiny non-identifiable workers seen at a distance, no portraits. The boats, quay and warehouses occupy a horizontal middle band that remains readable when cropped to a wide 3:1 banner. Calm jade harbor water across foreground, layered low tropical hills far away, warm ivory morning sky. Composition: harbor village left, largest boat center-right, graceful broad horizontal scene. Sophisticated hand-painted gouache and colored pencil on cream paper with delicate grain, refined restrained palette of sage, dusty teal, indigo gray, weathered timber and muted terracotta. Serene, spacious, credible editorial feeling, meticulously drawn ship rigging. Not photorealistic, not cartoon, no neon, no tech graphics, no glowing gradients. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark. This is conceptual original brand artwork, not a customer facility. Distinctly Indonesian architecture and phinisi form. Avoid volcano-centered river landscapes or rice terraces.

### Rice terraces prompt

One original refined editorial landscape illustration for an Indonesian business website's closing consultation banner. Wide panoramic aspect ratio 3:2, high resolution. Beautiful Indonesian rice terraces inspired by rural Java and Bali, viewed obliquely from a nearby hillside. Horizontal sculpted terraces following long graceful contours across rolling hills, irrigation glinting between sage green young rice rows, small shaded footpath leading toward a humble timber field pavilion on the RIGHT third beneath a cluster of tall coconut palms, distant misty hills and tiny red tiled village roofs. No river valley, no boats or harbor, no dramatic volcano. Composition deliberately quieter on the LEFT half with spacious softly textured rice fields, to sit behind a text overlay added later; strongest detail on right. Keep terrace contours and pavilion within the middle horizontal band so a broad website banner crop still makes sense. Afternoon light, warm ivory and muted golden highlights, deep forest green shadow, dusty sage and pale teal haze. Sophisticated hand-painted gouache and colored pencil, beautiful fine paper grain, delicate painterly vegetation and natural asymmetry. Premium calm editorial illustration, same quality as a finely illustrated travel book, not a photo, not cartoon, no stock corporate tech imagery. No text, no logo, no watermark. This is original conceptual Indonesian brand artwork. Landscape should feel intimately agricultural and human-scale, an entirely distinct scene from a coastal archipelago river.
